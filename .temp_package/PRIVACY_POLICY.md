# Privacy Policy for AiFly

**Last Updated: February 18, 2026**

## Overview

AiFly is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how AiFly handles your information.

## What Data We Collect

AiFly does **NOT** collect, store, or transmit any personal data to our servers.

### Data Stored Locally

- **Chat History**: Your conversations are stored entirely in your browser using Chrome's `storage.sync` API
- **API Keys**: Your OpenAI and Google Gemini API keys are stored locally in your browser's secure storage
- **User Preferences**: Font size, provider selection, and other settings are stored locally

## What Data Goes to Third Parties

When you use AiFly, your prompts and the AI's responses are sent directly to:

1. **OpenAI** (if using ChatGPT):
   - Your API key is sent with each request
   - Your prompts and responses are subject to [OpenAI's Privacy Policy](https://openai.com/privacy)
2. **Google** (if using Google Gemini):
   - Your API key is sent with each request
   - Your prompts and responses are subject to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

**Important**: You must review the privacy policies of OpenAI and Google, as AiFly does not control how they handle your data.

## What We Do NOT Do

- ❌ We do not collect your API keys
- ❌ We do not store your conversations on any server
- ❌ We do not track your browsing activity
- ❌ We do not sell or share your information
- ❌ We do not show ads or analytics
- ❌ We do not have access to any data beyond what you input

## Permissions Explained

AiFly requests the following permissions:

- **storage**: To save your chat history, settings, and API keys locally in your browser
- **scripting**: To inject the messenger interface and run the extension on web pages
- **activeTab**: To know which tab is currently active
- **\<all_urls\>**: To run on any website (so you can access the messenger from anywhere)

All permissions are used exclusively to provide the extension's core functionality.

## Data Security

- All your data is stored locally on your device
- Your browser handles encryption of stored data
- Your API keys are never transmitted to AiFly servers (we don't have servers)
- Each API request goes directly to OpenAI or Google from your browser

## Your Control

You can:

- **Delete chat history**: Use the "Clear" button in the messenger
- **Remove API keys**: Delete them from the Options page
- **Disable the extension**: Uninstall it anytime
- **Clear browser storage**: Use Chrome's settings to clear extension data

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can:

- Review the open-source code on [GitHub](https://github.com)
- Open an issue on the repository

## Changes to This Policy

AiFly may update this privacy policy. We will notify users of any material changes.

## Compliance

AiFly is designed to comply with:

- Chrome Web Store Privacy Requirements
- GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)
- CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act)
- Other privacy laws in your jurisdiction

Since all data is stored locally and no data is collected by AiFly, your privacy is fully protected.
